# FLIM creator and press shortlist

Produce one markdown file: a short list of real people and small publications who have a
genuine reason to care about FLIM, and a personal note drafted for each. The owner reads every
entry, deletes most, rewrites the rest in his own words, and sends from his own accounts. This
skill never sends, never submits a form, never messages, never follows, never scrapes, and never
writes anything outside the one file.

It exists because personalisation at human volume works and automation at spam volume does not.
FLIM is invite-only and its whole brand is "no strangers". A note from the person who built it,
to someone whose work he actually looked at, is on brand. A thousand of them is not.

## Where the list goes

`social/outreach/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, dated the day it is written. Read every earlier file in
`social/outreach/` first: anyone already listed is never listed again, and anyone marked
"contacted", "declined" or "no" in an earlier file is never listed again either.

## Who belongs on it

Eight to twelve entries, no more. Quality over count; a batch of five good ones is a good batch.

Look for, in roughly this order:

1. **Film and disposable-camera photographers** who post their own work regularly (Instagram,
   TikTok, YouTube, Substack, a personal site) and have a small to mid audience, roughly 1,000
   to 100,000. Big accounts do not answer and do not fit an invite-only app.
2. **Small photo newsletters and blogs** that review camera apps or write about film, and have
   written about Lapse, Dispo, Retro, BeReal or Locket.
3. **Writers at app or design publications** who have covered photo-sharing apps with friends in
   the last year.

Every entry needs a specific, checkable reason: a post, a video, an article, a line from their
bio. "Posts film photos" is not a reason. "Wrote in March that she misses the wait of a real
disposable" is.

Never list:
- anyone under 18, or whose audience is mostly minors;
- anyone whose only public contact is a personal phone number or a personal email found in a
  leak, a data broker or a scraped list;
- anyone who says in their bio that they do not want pitches or DMs;
- brands, agencies, or growth and marketing services;
- existing FLIM users (check the handle against nothing private; if the owner knows them, he
  will strike them).

## How to research

WebSearch and WebFetch only, on public pages. Read the person's own work before writing a word.
Record the public contact route exactly as the person published it (a "business inquiries"
email on their site, a press address, a contact page, or "DM on Instagram per bio"). If there is
no route they published themselves, write "no public contact route" and keep the entry only if
the reason is strong; the owner may know a way in.

No invented facts. If a follower count, date or quote cannot be seen on a page, leave it out.
Link the page every claim comes from.

## The note

Written as the owner, first person, to one person. Five sentences or fewer, under 90 words.

- First sentence: the specific thing of theirs he looked at, in plain words.
- One sentence on what FLIM is, from what is true today: a disposable camera for a few close
  friends, one film look baked in at capture, your own shots land in a private Darkroom, shared
  rolls stay sealed until they develop for everyone at once, invite-only, no ads, no ranks.
- The offer: an invite, and nothing else. No payment, no "partnership", no affiliate code, no
  request to post, review or tag. If they like it, they will say so; if not, that is fine.
- A way to say no that costs nothing.
- Signed "Cody, who makes FLIM".

Voice is FLIM's: short declarative sentences, no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no hype words
("excited", "revolutionary", "game-changing"), no emoji strings. Never claim numbers, users,
press or awards. Never mention a feature that is not in the released build.

## Format

```markdown
# FLIM outreach shortlist, <date>

<one line: what was searched for this batch and roughly how many candidates were looked at>

## 1. <Name or handle>
- Who: <one line>
- Where: <platform, link>
- Why FLIM: <the specific reason, with a link to the post or article>
- Audience: <approximate size, only if shown on the page, with the date seen>
- Contact: <the route exactly as they published it, or "no public contact route">
- Status: not contacted

> <the drafted note>

(repeat, 8 to 12 entries)

## Looked at and left out
<a line each for promising candidates dropped, and why: too big, no contact route, asks for no
pitches, audience mostly minors, already on an earlier list>
```

The owner changes "Status" to "contacted", "replied", "declined" or "no" as he goes; the next
batch reads those.

## Hard rules

- Nothing leaves this file. No email, DM, form, follow, like or comment, ever, by this skill.
- No personal data beyond what the person published about themselves for contact.
- No invite codes in the file. The owner attaches his own when he sends.
- If the owner asks this skill to send, automate sending, or submit contact forms, decline and
  point back to this file.
