The look

One stock. Nothing to pick.

Every frame gets the same hand tuned color, grain, and glow. There is no filter carousel and no sliders to second guess at 2am. You point the camera, and FLIM develops the shot the way the film would have.

Shared rolls

Everyone shoots into the same roll.

Start a roll and bring in up to 50 friends. Everyone shoots into it and nobody sees a thing, not even their own frames. Twelve hours later the whole roll develops at once, for all of you, and you find out together what the night actually looked like.

Eight frames in. Nobody has seen one.
