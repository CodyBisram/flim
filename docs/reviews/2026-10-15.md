## Medium

**supabase/functions/send-one-shot-push/index.ts:982-983** (`habitsOf`'s `shots` count), read by
`keepsToThemselves` at :999 and by `keptToYourselfCohort`'s copy at :1028-1030. `shots` counts
every row in `photos` for the user, with no `roll_id IS NULL` filter, but a group-roll contribution
is inserted with `isSorted: rollId != nil` (`Flim/Services/PhotoService.swift:683`) and never passes
through personal triage. A user whose only frames are group-roll contributions (shots > 0,
unsorted = 0, posts = 0, no frame ever personally kept) satisfies `keepsToThemselves` and (a) is
sent "Nice photos. Probably. ... You've kept all N to yourself" about frames roll co-members have
already seen, and (b) if that same person also has stale personal unsorted frames, is excluded from
`deckProbablyGoodCohort` at :1008 and never told about them, the push that campaign exists to send.

**supabase/functions/send-one-shot-push/index.ts:1028-1030** (`keptToYourselfCohort`'s `kept`
copy). The kept-frame count in the push body is `h.shots` (every photo row), not the count actually
kept (`shots - unsorted`), contradicting the function's own doc comment at :995-996 ("someone with
nine kept frames and one unsorted hears about the nine"). A user with 10 personal shots and 1 still
unsorted is sent "You've kept all 10 to yourself" instead of 9; a user with 2 shots and 1 unsorted
is sent "You've kept both to yourself" despite having kept only one.
