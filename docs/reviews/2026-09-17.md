# Review notes for Sep 17

Review of commits from the last 24 hours (c7dac48..2313c54).

## 1. Flim/Services/FeedSeenStore.swift:71-77 (activeUserId didSet drops unsynced marks)

`activeUserId`'s `didSet` resets `pendingSync` to `[:]` (or cancels `flushTask` and leaves it, in the sign-out branch) on every account switch or sign-out, with no attempt to flush the outgoing account's unsent marks first. `ContentView.swift:131` and `:175` set `activeUserId` (on account switch and on sign-out respectively) without calling `FeedSeenStore.shared.flushPending()` beforehand, unlike `UndoCenter.shared.flush()` two lines above at :117, which is deliberately flushed first "before any cache reset below" for exactly this reason (see its own comment).

Failure scenario: a signed-in person swipes through a day's photos, marking several posts seen locally (`markSeen` persists them to this device immediately and queues them in `pendingSync`, debounced 4 seconds before `flushPending` sends them to `post_seen`). Within that 4-second window (or during the network round trip of an in-flight flush), they sign out to test something, or switch to a second account on the same device (both real flows: sign-out is common in this alpha for onboarding tests, and the seed list in `FeedSeenSeed.keptFullyUnseen` names four testers by id). `activeUserId`'s `didSet` fires synchronously, clearing `pendingSync` (or cancelling the pending `flushTask`) before those marks ever reach `post_seen`. The marks stay correct forever on this one device (`seenAt` was already persisted to `UserDefaults`), but the account's server copy never gets them. A reinstall, a second phone, or the seven-day server-side `feed_unseen_count()` on another device will show those specific posts as unseen again — the exact "16 shots from 8 friends already read" symptom this whole feature (`post_seen`, `pullFromServer`, tonight's commit) was built to fix, just reached through account-switch timing instead of reinstall.

Severity: medium.
