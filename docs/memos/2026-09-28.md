# Product memo, week of Sep 28

## The number

Posts and photos nearly doubled this week (posts 111 to 193, photos 301 to 488) and shooters over
openers rose 36% to 43%, the best conversion in a month. But reciprocal_pairs_7d kept sliding, 33
to 30, still well off the ~50 seen two weeks ago. More people are shooting; the mutual engagement
that makes this a social app is not following.

## What shipped, what it did

- Spotlight (long-press a frame to put it up for weekly curation) was built across a dozen
  commits this week, but 1.6.0 has not been submitted. Users are still on 1.5.3. No signal yet.
- Sort deck and Spotlight sheet layout fixes (posted notice, the swap rule) are the same story:
  real code, no released surface for it yet.
- The shot-this-week Spotlight restriction went into the database, but only matters once 1.6.0
  ships and the feature is reachable.
- Outreach email sending moved off the Gmail connector to direct SMTP, since the connector was
  rewriting invite links into an expiring Google redirect. Operational, not in-app; invites_redeemed
  held flat at 4, too soon to tell.

## Where users leak

- App Store data: not enough yet.
- Accounts to openers is still the widest gap: 76 accounts, a 27 average daily openers.
- Reciprocal pairs sit at 30 against 76 accounts; the social layer is thinner than headcount implies.
