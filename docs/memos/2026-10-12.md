# Weekly product memo, 2026-10-12

## The number

Reciprocal pairs (who's reacting to whom, the social layer) fell from 27 to 21 this week even
as accounts grew from 81 to 85. That's the third weekly reading in a row going the wrong way:
35 two weeks ago, 27 last week, 21 now. The app keeps adding people who aren't pairing up.

## What shipped, what it did

- 1.6.2 went live around 10-06: three badges, offline capture, read-state that stays read, an
  iOS 26 visual refresh. No signal in reciprocal pairs, shooters, or openers this week.
- Friday's Spotlight push (10-09): shooters and photos jumped the next day, 7 to 13 and 46 to
  91. Consistent with prior weeks, still just two data points.
- The inviter-nudge banner built 10-06, meant to prompt a new account to react to whoever
  invited them, is still sitting unreleased in 1.6.3. It hasn't had a chance to help yet.
- Invite-code hardening and six Darkroom/upload fixes landed 10-10, but the migration is
  unapplied and the client code unreleased. Nothing to credit them with.

## Where users leak

- Impressions held flat around 180; product page views nearly halved, 64 to 29; first-time
  downloads fell to 4 from 15. The top of funnel got thinner, not weaker.
- Every one of those 4 downloads became an account this week, versus about a third the week
  before. The invite gate isn't where this week's loss happened.
- Inside the app the leak is social: openers eased only slightly (29 to 27) but reciprocal
  pairs kept falling, so people open, some even shoot, and don't react to anyone.

## This week, do one of these

1. Ship the 1.6.3 inviter-nudge banner that's already built and sitting on main since 10-06.
   It's the one change in the pipeline aimed straight at this number. Metric: reciprocal_pairs_7d;
   check in two weeks whether the 35-27-21 slide stops.
2. Keep the Friday Spotlight push as a standing routine, and fix the open bug where a last-card
   publish failure is silently swallowed (SortDeckView.swift, open since 09-27), so the one
   lever that visibly moves shooters stops losing submissions. Metric: shooters the two days
   after each send, and Spotlight publish failures.
3. Fix the daily-digest bug where the same tail of recipients past the run's time budget never
   gets a digest (open since 10-11). A daily push is a cheap pull on lapsing openers, and right
   now one slice of users never gets it. Metric: openers_7d_avg for that recipient tail.

## Watch

- First-time downloads and page views are at their lowest point since this data started, three
  releases into 1.6.x with no visible lift to the top of funnel yet.
- new_accounts has been zero for two straight days (10-09, 10-10) with founding_left down to
  15. Not a week-long trend yet, worth tracking given how few seats are left.
- Shooters-to-openers conversion is soft for a second week running, the same item flagged last
  week. Not this week's biggest problem, but not improving either.
