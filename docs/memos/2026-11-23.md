## Where users leak

- Impressions to page views to first-time downloads: 90 to 33 to 3 this week, versus 70 to 16
  to 4 the week before. Discovery is up; the invite wall right after it is working as designed.
- Openers to shooters: 54 of 141 opens became a shot, 38%, the same band as last week's 37%.
  The steadiest drop anywhere in the funnel.
- Posts to reciprocal pairs: 122 posts against only 22 reciprocal pairs this week, down from
  112 posts and 29 pairs the week before. The interaction loop is the step getting thinner.
