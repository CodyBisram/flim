# Weekly product memo, 2026-10-19

## The number

Reciprocal pairs, the clearest read on whether friends still react to each other, fell to 9
this week from 23 the week before, the lowest reading since this sheet started. That is the
same slide last week's memo flagged, now worse, while the fix built for it still has not shipped.

## What shipped, what it did

- Open door (sign up with no code, owner-switched) and three one-shot push nudges went live
  server-side around Oct 15. open_door has logged zero signups every day since.
- The daily digest now fires at each person's local noon instead of a fixed window.
  openers_7d_avg kept falling after the change, 26 to 20. No signal yet.
- Spotlight-stays-in-view, the new notification sound, and the invite "Follow back" push are
  built (build 437) but not released. No app_release_gate entry exists for 1.6.3 yet, so none
  of this week's numbers can be credited to them.
- Server-side push and session-race fixes also shipped. Nothing in NUMBERS.md moves on a race fix.

## Where users leak

- The App Store funnel is small and shrinking (impressions 180 to 64, page views 31 to 4 week
  over week), but that is by design here, not the growth lever for an invite-only app.
- Among the 85 existing accounts, opens fell from 183 to 141 and shooters from 72 to 47; the
  open-to-shoot rate slipped from 39% to 33%.
- New accounts and invites redeemed both read zero on all seven days this week, down from 3
  and 4 the week before. The one public door in produced nothing.

## This week, do one of these

1. Submit and release build 437 (1.6.3): the invite follow-back push and Spotlight presence
   have sat idle since Oct 14. Metric: openers_7d_avg and reciprocal_pairs_7d, two weeks out.
2. Have the daily digest name one friend's post the reader has not reacted to, reusing its own
   recipient loop. Metric: reciprocal_pairs_7d finding a floor within two weeks.
3. Prompt the 85 existing accounts to send an invite now that invites_redeemed sits at zero.
   Metric: invites_redeemed and new_accounts over the next two weeks.
