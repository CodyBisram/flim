# Weekly memo, 2026-11-09

## The number

Reciprocal pairs (trailing 7 days) fell from 26 on Oct 31 to 7 on Nov 7, a steady slide over
eight straight daily readings. Accounts held flat at 87 and openers averaged about 21-22 a day
in both weeks, so people are still opening the app, they are just not reacting or commenting on
each other anymore.

## What shipped, what it did

- FLIM Beta (a separate TestFlight app, not the live App Store build) got new sign-in, roll,
  Friends, and notification-ask screens on its "desk" redesign. No production signal expected or
  seen; none of the 70 accounts are on it.
- FLIM Beta's follow pushes switched to "friends" wording, deployed to FLIM Dev test accounts
  only. Same: not visible to anyone counted in NUMBERS.md.
- The nightly review opened two medium findings in the V2 Darkroom and Rolls screens; both are
  beta-only code paths with no live-app exposure.
- Nothing shipped to the production build (1.6.3) this week. A 1.6.4 version bump sits in
  project.yml but has not been submitted.

## Where users leak

- Impressions to page views: 112 to 20 this week versus 167 to 38 the week before, over 80%
  lost at that step in both windows.
- Downloads to accounts: 5 first-time downloads and 0 invites redeemed or new accounts this
  week, against 2 downloads and 3 redemptions the week before. The invite wall is the block now,
  not App Store interest.
- Openers to reciprocal pairs: openers barely moved, but reciprocal_pairs_7d fell from 26 to 7,
  the sharpest drop anywhere in the funnel.
