## Where users leak

- Impressions to page views to first-time downloads: 58 to 8 to 1 this week, versus 99 to 27 to
  8 the week before. Organic discovery is not the growth engine here; the app is invite-only.
- Invites redeemed to new accounts: 2 to 2 this week, nearly 1 to 1. The bottleneck is how few
  invites go out, not what happens once one is used.
- Openers to shooters: 58 of 156 opens turned into a shot this week, 37%, about the same as 36%
  the week before. This is the single biggest drop anywhere in the funnel.
