# Weekly product memo, 2026-10-26

## The number

Reciprocal pairs, the clearest read on whether the social layer is alive, nearly doubled: 9 (week
ending Oct 18) to 18 (week ending Oct 25). Openers' 7-day average moved the same direction, 20 to
22. Both had been sliding since mid-September; this is the first week either turned up.

## What shipped, what it did

- The site's icon, favicon and link-preview images were replaced (Oct 24). Web only, no app
  metric tracks it, no signal expected.
- 1.6.3's user-facing work, notification reactions, the invite reminder line, Spotlight closing
  fixes, has been code-complete since the Oct 21-23 copy approval but is still unsubmitted.
  Nothing it touches can move until it ships.
- The rest of the week's commits were nightly automation (NUMBERS.md, ASC pulls) and review
  ledger entries. No other user-visible change landed.

## Where users leak

- Page views to first downloads is the sharpest drop: 12 page views, 1 first-time download in the
  latest full App Store week (Oct 18-24), versus 4 and 2 the week before.
- Openers to shooters: 152 opens, 59 shooters last week (39%), close to the prior week's 141 and
  47 (33%).
- Posts to reciprocal pairs: 122 posts against 18 reciprocal pairs last week, activity still runs
  well ahead of two-way connection.

   notification reactions. Metric: invites_redeemed and reactions. Check in two weeks against this

## Watch

- Impressions are flat to down, 55 last full App Store week versus 62 the week before, off a small
  base.
- Growth is stalled: accounts moved only 85 to 87 in a week, with Founding 100 nearly gone.
- Reactions are bursty: one day, Oct 25, carried 71 of the week's reactions and 163 of its photos,
  so engagement still rides on a handful of sessions rather than a broad base.
