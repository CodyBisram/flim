# Weekly product memo, 2026-10-05

## The number

Accounts went from 76 to 81 this week (5 new, versus 2 the week before). First-time App Store
downloads jumped from 3 to 15 over the same two weeks, but only about a third of those
downloads turned into an account.

## What shipped, what it did

- 1.6.0 (live 09-29): Spotlight weekly feature, feed read-state, invites that replenish on an
  invitee's first photo. New accounts jumped by 4 that day; invites redeemed rose to 7 from 3.
- 1.6.1 (live 09-29/30): flash fix, clearer upload status, offline error messaging. No signal
  yet in shooters or photos.
- Two weekend Spotlight pushes (sent 10-02, reaching 23 and 15 accounts): shooters went 7, 18,
  26 and photos went 35, 140, 173 over the next two days. Likely signal, though weekend timing
  alone could explain part of it.
- 1.6.2 (Darkroom and red-dot fixes) is submitted, not yet live; nothing to credit it with.

## Where users leak

- Page views to downloads: 64 product page views produced 15 first-time downloads, about
  23 percent, versus 30 views and 3 downloads the week before.
- Downloads to accounts: of those 15 downloads, only 5 accounts were created. Expected for
  invite-only, but it caps what store traffic alone can do.
- Openers to shooters softened to 41 percent (82 of 200) from 45 percent (81 of 181): more
  people open without picking up the camera.

## This week, do one of these

1. Keep the weekly Spotlight push running every week, not as a one-off. It is the only lever
   that visibly moved shooters and photos this week. Metric: shooters over openers; check in
   two weeks whether it holds above this week's 41 percent once routine.
2. Add a capture step at the invite wall: a first-time download that opens without a code gets
   a share-sheet prompt back to whoever referred them. Metric: download-to-account rate, about
   a third this week; check in two weeks for a clear rise.
3. Prompt new accounts to follow back or react to their inviter in the first session.
   Reciprocal pairs fell from 35 to 27 this week even as accounts grew. Metric:
   reciprocal_pairs_7d; check in two weeks that the decline has stopped.

## Watch

- Reciprocal pairs down to 27 from 35 a week ago while accounts grew, the social graph
  thinning relative to headcount (27 of 81 versus 35 of 76).
- Openers-to-shooters softening above is still small; worth confirming it is not the start of
  a longer drift.
- Much of this week's App Store search impressions came from countries (Thailand, Russia,
  China, Nigeria, and others) with almost no matching downloads, traffic the invite gate
  cannot convert.
