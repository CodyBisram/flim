# Product memo, week of Sep 14

## The number

Two days of data only, the job started 2026-09-12. Biggest move: follows dropped 71 to 5, day
over day, the same day followers-only posts reached every live client.

## What shipped, what it did

- 1.5.2 (one-screen sign-up, Find friends) went live 09-11. No pre-release numbers exist to
  compare against, so no signal yet.

  redeemed). Both fell near zero by 09-13.

## Where users leak

  biggest drop.
- Openers to shooters: 33 opened, 17 shot. About half who open never touch the camera.
- Invites redeemed: 5 on 09-12, 0 on 09-13, tracking new_accounts exactly. FLIM's only public
  door produced zero arrivals.
