# Product memo, week of Sep 21

## The number

reciprocal_pairs_7d, the best proxy for a living social layer, fell from 47 to 33 over the past
week, even as accounts grew 70 to 74. Headcount is up, mutual engagement is down.

## What shipped, what it did

- 1.5.3 went fully live 09-15 (build 378): one-tap follow-up rolls, roll-code sign-up, page
  limited to followers. Shooters peaked at 16 on 09-19, the week's best day, but reciprocal
  pairs kept falling through it. No clean signal either way.
- BALI26, a 24-seat promo cohort code good through 09-24, shipped 09-17. New accounts ran 1, 0,
  2, 0 the four days after. No signal yet.
- "Start another with this group" (`start_follow_up_roll`) has been live since 1.5.3 and genuinely
  inserts a row into `rolls`. rolls_created still read zero every day this week, and the two
  before it.
- The numbers job's silent-drop bug (a failed repair step could eat a night's row) was fixed
  09-15. This is the first full week collected without that risk.

## Where users leak

- App Store data: not enough yet.
- Accounts to openers is the widest gap: 74 accounts, a 26 average daily openers. Just over a
  third of the roster opens FLIM on a given day.
- Openers to shooters: 182 opens and 66 shooters this week. Two of every three opens end with no
  photo taken.

## This week, do one of these

1. Find out why rolls_created has read zero for nine straight days despite `start_follow_up_roll`
   and `CreateRollView` both being live paths that write to `rolls`. Either the button isn't
   getting tapped or something upstream of the insert is failing silently. Watch rolls_created
   for any nonzero day within two weeks.
2. Prompt people already holding unused invites to use them before the Founding 100 runs out (26
   seats left, down 4 this week); it is friend-to-friend, not public. Watch invites_redeemed
   (4 this week) for a rise within two weeks.
3. Look at whether the "your page is for the people who follow you" and posting-audience changes
   made people post more cautiously to old friends. Watch reciprocal_pairs_7d for a turn back up
   from 33 within two weeks.

## Watch

- openers_7d_avg slid 28 to 26 across the week; the DAU proxy is drifting down while accounts
  grow.
- Founding 100 is down to 26 seats; at this week's pace they run out in about six weeks.
- edge_timeouts_24h swung from 2 to 18 with no fix in view. Not touching the numbers above yet.
