# FLIM v2: the game plan, second attempt (2026-09-19)

The first v2 (Claude Design, "Stage 1 design directions", 2026-09-13 to 14) was built as far as
batch 1 (foundations plus the everyday journey), batch 2 (camera permission, comments over
Activity) and batch 4a (cohort arrivals land on Find friends). The owner tested batch 1 on
TestFlight 379 and did not like the redesign as a whole; the pieces that were right came over to
1.5.4 one at a time (the sort vocabulary, the capture chip, the audience sentence, the type
tokens, the two behaviours). The feed card's new shape, the labelled React/Comment row and the
inset photograph stayed on the branch. This document is what v2 should be the second time.

## What the first attempt got wrong

1. It redesigned the surface before the study ran. The launch tab, the tab order and the
   Activity unread contract were all proposed as design, when each is a measurable question.
2. It made the photograph smaller (33pt inset, radius 6) to make room for chrome. On a photo
   app the photograph is the point; the owner saw it and felt the loss before he could name it.
3. It replaced one-tap reactions with a labelled React button and a tray. Correct on paper
   (labels, 44pt targets), wrong for a product whose loved behaviour is a thumb landing on a
   heart. Reactions are 97 percent of responses; every extra tap on that path is felt.
4. Its Nocturne design system (blurple accent, Inter, outlined buttons) was a design-board
   identity, not FLIM's. FLIM is amber, near-black, film grain and a burned-in date. The
   prototype looked like a different app and the boards said so themselves.
5. It was delivered as seven clickable HTML boards. Beautiful, but the owner cannot hold them
   next to the real app on his phone, which is the only place he judges anything.

## What must not change (owner decisions, standing)

Camera-first launch until the study says otherwise. Tab order as it is. Activity's watermark.
The inviter auto-follow at sign-up. The film look, byte-identical since 1.5.0. The 3:4 frame,
no exceptions. One-tap default reactions. No em dashes anywhere. No scores, streaks, ranks, or
public counts. Followers-only reads plus tags. The first-sort Darkroom landing for a new account.

## What v2 is for, this time

Not a new look. A tightening of the one FLIM has, in service of the everyday loop: see a
friend's day, answer it, shoot one of your own, get answered, come back. The measure is
reciprocal pairs (54 this week) and the share of the 100 who open on an ordinary Tuesday.

## The plan, in the order it should run

**Phase 0, the study (owner, one afternoon, before anything is drawn).** docs/USABILITY_STUDY.md,
five people, three doors, seven tasks. It settles the launch tab, the tab order, whether the
strip is found, and whether people know who sees their post. Design starts from its sheets.

**Phase 1, foundations (already in 1.5.4).** FlimTheme roles, FlimSpace, FlimRadius, FlimType.
Nothing to do; the next screens are built on them.

**Phase 2, the feed card, redrawn on the real card.** Keep the photograph at its current width
and radius 12. Keep the emoji chips as one-tap. Make Comment a labelled control beside the chips
(it is the one response that is invisible today, at 3 percent). Keep the strip from two frames
(the owner liked the "1 of 2" cue; decide on device, not on a board). Every change as a toggle
in the demo host so the owner sees before and after on his phone.

**Phase 3, the page (own and others).** The identity header trimmed, chapters as a row with a
route, the first post's home, the invite code where a push can land on it (done in 1.5.4). No
chosen cover until someone asks.

**Phase 4, rolls.** The roll loop is stopped (no roll since Sep 6). Design the four card states
(waiting, ready, opened, full) and the prompt that starts a roll from an occasion the app can
see (a burst of shots in one place, three friends shooting the same evening). The reveal stays.

**Phase 5, the launch decision.** Only after the study. If it says Feed, ship Feed-first with the
resume rule and the one dismissible line; if it says Camera, close the question.

**Phase 6, trust and settings, then the supporter plan.** Report, block, delete flows redrawn
on the tokens; then the plan that funds storage: what free always includes is written in the
first v2's monetization appendix and stands.

## How the work is delivered

Not as HTML boards. As SwiftUI, on the v2 branch, one screen per PR-sized commit, each toggled
in FeedPreviewDemoHost so a TestFlight build shows old and new side by side. Claude Design is
used for exploration and the copy inventory, then the developer session builds from its boards
with the conflict list as the contract. The owner judges every step on his phone.
