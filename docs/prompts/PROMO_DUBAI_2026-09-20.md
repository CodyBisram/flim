# Prompt for Claude Design: the Dubai promo

Attach `dubai-promo-photos.zip` (six PNGs, 3024 by 4032, upright) and the Italy and Bali cards
as the reference for the series. Paste everything below the line.

---

You are making the third card in a series of Instagram promos for FLIM, an invite-only iPhone
app that is a disposable camera for your friends. The first two are attached: "Italy,
developed." and the Bali card. This one is Dubai. Same series, same voice, same restraint. It
has to sit in the grid beside the other two and read as the third of a set, not a new
campaign.

## What FLIM is, in one breath

You shoot, the photograph gets one film look baked in at capture, at 3:4, and it lands in your
Darkroom. You post it to your page and only the people who follow you see it. A roll is a
shared camera for one occasion that develops for everyone at once. Invite only. No filters,
no scores, no ads. Near-black ground, one warm amber accent, SF Pro, an orange seven-segment
date stamp on exports. The photograph is the only colour on any surface.

## The six photographs

All shot on an iPhone in Dubai, all portrait 3:4, in this order in the zip:

1. Dinner under the Burj Khalifa at night: a plate in the foreground, a pink table lamp, the
   tower filling the frame behind. The hero, if you use only one.
2. The Burj Al Arab lit teal, from the beach, reflected in black water. Night.
3. The Marina from the bow of a boat: white rail, dark water, a wall of towers under haze.
   Daylight.
4. An orange Lamborghini Spyder on herringbone paving, the Ain Dubai wheel and the skyline
   behind, a yellow line on the ground. Daylight, hazy.
5. Sheikh Zayed Road from a car at sunset, towers either side, the road running to a vanishing
   point, the hood of the car at the bottom of the frame.
6. Atlantis The Palm from high up at dusk, the arch on the horizon, the Palm's spine and the
   villas below, traffic on the trunk road.

Two of them carry the day (3, 4), two the golden hour (5, 6), two the night (1, 2). That is
the arc. Use it.

## What to make

Three candidates, one board each, all 4:5 at 1080 by 1350 for the Instagram feed, plus a
9:16 story crop of the one you would pick:

- **A. One frame.** Photograph 1 alone, full bleed on the near-black ground with the card's
  margin, the line set small in the lower left, the date stamp in the lower right. The
  quietest possible card. This is the shape the Italy card takes.
- **B. The roll.** All six as a contact strip or a 2 by 3 sheet on the ground, ordered
  day to night, with the line under it. The card that says "a whole trip, shared once".
- **C. Day and night.** Two frames side by side, 4 (the car, day) and 1 (the dinner, night),
  the line across the seam. The card with the most tension.

For every candidate: the FLIM ground (#0A0A0A), SF Pro only, the amber (#FABD5C) at most
once and as a mark (the stamp, a hairline, a single word), the photographs untouched except
for a crop that keeps 3:4 per frame. No gradient overlays, no stickers, no drop shadows, no
device mockup, no app screenshots, no logos other than the word FLIM set in SF Pro. Type
never sits over a face or over the tower's spire.

## The line

Lead with this, exactly, as the Italy card did:

> Dubai, developed.

Below it, one of these, or a better one in the same register (plain, short, no exclamation
marks, no em dashes, no "capture", "moments", "memories", "vibes"):

- Six frames. One trip. Shared once.
- Shot on FLIM. Developed at home.
- Everyone on the trip shot the same roll.
- Nothing posted until it developed.

The audience is people who might know someone on FLIM. The last line on every card is the
same as the other two: "If you know someone on FLIM, ask them for an invite." Set it at the
smallest size that stays readable at phone scale, and never larger than the line above it.

## What not to do

- Do not stylize the photographs to look like film. They are iPhone captures and the card
  is honest about that; the film look belongs to the app, and the owner may run them through
  it himself before posting. If you want to suggest a treatment, say so in a note, do not
  apply it.
- Do not add people, a phone, a hand, a plane ticket, a map, a passport, a flag, Arabic
  calligraphy, or gold. Dubai is in the photographs already.
- Do not crop the Burj Khalifa's spire or the Burj Al Arab's mast.
- Do not use more than two typographic sizes on a card.
- Do not centre everything. The Italy card is left-aligned; match it.

## Deliver

The three 4:5 boards, the one 9:16 story, and beneath each board two lines: why this crop,
and what you would change if the owner said "quieter". Then the caption for the post, under
40 words, ending with the invite line, with at most five hashtags, chosen from:
#filmphotography #disposablecamera #dubai #35mm #photography #shotonflim.

The owner posts from his phone and judges the card in the Instagram grid beside the Italy
and Bali cards. Draw for that comparison.
