# FLIM outreach shortlist, 2026-09-25

Searched for film and disposable-camera newsletters on Substack, reviews of Lapse, and 2026 coverage of Retro, Locket and Dispo. About a dozen candidates came up. None made the list: every candidate page (and substack.com, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Reddit and Wikipedia) was blocked by this session's network proxy. That means none of their work could be read and no reason, audience or contact route could be checked. The skill requires all three, so this batch has no entries. The leads below are for a run that can open these pages.
