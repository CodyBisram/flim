# FLIM outreach shortlist, 2026-09-26

Started from the eleven leads in 2026-09-25.md, then searched for 2026 disposable-camera essays on Substack, Camp Snap and single-use camera reviews, and recent press on Retro, Locket and Lapse. About 27 candidates looked at, nine made the list. Pages read on 2026-09-25.

- Where: Substack, https://eclectachrome.substack.com/
- Why FLIM: In "The OG Social Media for Film Photographers" (2026-07-05) she praises Lomography's community for "no ads, no algorithms" and writes "you are not the product, you are a community member" ([post](https://eclectachrome.substack.com/p/the-og-social-media-for-film-photographers)). Her 2026 cameras include an Instax Wide Evo and a Pentax 17 ([About](https://eclectachrome.substack.com/about)).
- Audience: not shown on the pages read.
- Contact: no email published. Her Linktree lists Instagram @eclectachrome, TikTok @eclectachrome and YouTube ([Linktree](https://linktr.ee/eclectachrome)).
- Status: not contacted

> I read your July post on Lomography as the original social media for film photographers, and the line about no ads and no algorithms stuck with me.
