# FLIM social drafts, week of Aug 26

Covers the Darkroom redesign (three zoom distances, film strips per night, month paging),
the save-to-camera-roll-after-reveal work, and the feed's morning behavior. All of it is
on the unreleased train, so every post frames it as in progress.

## X

### 1. The 04:00 boundary
> Building FLIM's feed taught us that midnight is the wrong place to end a day. A night out runs 11:40 to 12:20 and it is one night, not two. So a day in FLIM ends at 4am. A photo taken at 2am files under yesterday, which is what the person who took it would call it.

Why now: capture-time narration and the day boundary landed across the feed and Darkroom this cycle, and it is the most explainable design decision in the pile.

### 2. Film strips per night (thread, 3 posts)
> We are rebuilding FLIM's Darkroom. The old version was a grid. The new one gives every night its own strip of film.
>
> Zoom out once and a month shows only the frames it filled. Zoom out again and each month leads with the shot its owner loved most. Three distances, one archive.
>
> None of this is a feed. It is your own shelf of negatives, and nobody ranks it.

Why now: the three-distance zoom, month covers, and filled-frames work all landed this fortnight; this is the centerpiece of the next release.

### 3. Photos that walk themselves home
> New in the next FLIM build: shots you keep can save to your camera roll on their own. Roll photos wait until you have watched the reveal, because the reveal is the point.

Why now: the save-on-develop toggle and its reveal gate were finished this cycle.

### 4. Mornings
> A photo app you open every morning should notice that it is morning. We spent this week making FLIM's feed reset the way a fresh day deserves: read days clear out, what arrived overnight is waiting at the top, and the count in the header only ever tells the truth.

Why now: the morning boundary and ledger fixes went in today; honest build-in-public material without naming them as bug fixes.

## Skipped on purpose

- Anything about performance work, caching, or reliability fixes: reads as internal
  plumbing, and framing fixes publicly invites "what was broken" questions.
- The follow count and profile badge work: badges are a retention surface, and posting
  about them reads as gamification, which the brand voice refuses.
- Invite mechanics: the list is deliberately closed right now, so no post should create
  demand the door cannot absorb.
