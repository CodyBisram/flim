# FLIM first promo posts, 2026-09-07

Two Instagram posts, one photograph each, from a member's Bali honeymoon, shot in FLIM. This is
the first time a real member's photographs go outward-facing, which the asset rules in
`.claude/skills/social-drafts/SKILL.md` normally forbid. They go out only with the member's
written consent, and the consent is kept.

## Before posting

1. Ask the member, in writing, and keep the reply. Suggested text:
   > Hey, your Bali photos are the best things anyone has shot in FLIM. Can I use the sunset
   > daiquiri and the pink courtyard as the first two photos on the FLIM Instagram? Full credit
   > or no name, your call, and I will take either down any time you ask.
2. Their answer decides the credit line: named handle, first name only, or no credit.
3. The files are on the Desktop as `flim-ig-1-courtyard.jpg` and `flim-ig-2-sunset.jpg`,
   1080 by 1350, which is the tallest frame Instagram allows. The 3:4 master lost 6% of its
   height to a centred crop, sky and leaves at the top, tile and table at the bottom, nothing
   that mattered.
4. Do not let Instagram crop further. When the frame appears, tap the expand control so the
   whole 4:5 is used.
