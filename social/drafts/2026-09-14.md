# FLIM social drafts, week of Sep 14

Covers 1.5.2 going live (Find friends' reasons, the full emoji set, save-all-you're-in
export) and what's building for 1.5.3 (follow-up rolls, a roll code that gets a friend in,
a shot that is safe the instant it is taken).

## X

### 1. Find friends says why
> Find friends now says why each person showed up: people who follow you, people in your rolls, the friend who invited you, the people they invited. No strangers padding the list.

Why now: shipped in 1.5.2, live now; a small change that removes the guesswork from a screen everyone opens once and forgets.

### 2. Every emoji, for real
> The reaction picker used to only draw the emoji your font happened to have. Now it carries every emoji your iPhone knows, flags and joined emoji included.

Why now: shipped in 1.5.2; a small annoyance quietly fixed, the kind of thing you only notice once it's gone.

### 3. A shot is safe the instant you take it
> Building: a photo will write to your phone the moment you take it, before it waits its turn to upload, and finish uploading even if you lock the screen right after. If FLIM could not keep a shot, it will say so.

Why now: reliability work heading into 1.5.3, concrete about the exact failure mode it closes.

### 4. Start another with this group
> Building: when a roll's reveal ends, one tap will start the next one with the same group. Everyone gets a card with a clear "not this time," so nobody gets pulled into a roll they didn't choose.

Why now: follow-up rolls are coming in 1.5.3, and the opt-out is worth explaining before it ships.
