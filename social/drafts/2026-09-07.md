# FLIM social drafts, week of Sep 7

Covers the Chapters launch (now live), rolls getting steadier (ordering, burst stacking, a
viewer that holds a whole roll), and notifications landing where they say. All of this
shipped in the release that just went out, so it is framed as available, not coming.

## X

### 1. Chapters is live
> Chapters is live in FLIM. On the first of the month, the month you shared shows up on your page. It plays like a reveal: frames chosen on your phone, then the month in numbers. You decide which of those numbers other people see.

Why now: Chapters shipped in the release that just went out; this is the headline of the cycle.

### 2. A burst is one frame until you ask
> A burst of near identical shots used to just sit there as five almost-the-same photos. Now they stack into one frame. Tap it to fan it open. The reveal plays the sharpest one and leaves a note about the rest.

Why now: burst stacking and the fan-open gesture landed this cycle and is easy to feel the first time you see it.

### 3. Notifications that open the right thing
> A comment on a roll photo now opens that photo, thread included. A reaction opens the photo. Anything that reaches your phone as a push also shows up in your activity, including threads you joined but did not start.

Why now: notification routing was rebuilt this cycle; small, concrete, the kind of fix people actually notice.

### 4. Rolls hold their order now
> A roll's photos open in the order they were actually taken, not the order the server happened to store them in. And the viewer stopped giving up partway through a big roll. It holds the whole thing now.

Why now: ordering and viewer capacity both landed this cycle; unglamorous, but it is the difference between a roll feeling finished and feeling broken.

## Instagram

### 2. One tap, five frames
Asset: short screen-capture clip (under 10s), seeded demo content, showing a stacked
burst frame in a roll grid being tapped and fanning open into its individual shots.
> Five almost-identical shots. One frame until you ask.

Why now: motion sells the fan-open gesture better than a still, and it is new this cycle.

### 3. A chapter, shared
Asset: simulator screenshot of a chapter's contact sheet share view, seeded demo
content, no account details visible.
> Any month, as a contact sheet.

Why now: contact sheet sharing is part of the Chapters launch and reads as a clean, simple visual.

## Skipped on purpose

- The cross-account cache guard, the owner identity pin, and the join refusal's server
  side: security and integrity work with no user-facing story, same call the App Store
  copy itself made.
- The flash frame edge fix: it is a rendering artifact correction, not a change to the
  look, and reads as internal to anyone outside the team.
- The weekly database vacuum job: infrastructure housekeeping, not something to post
  about.
- Release candidate builds and version numbers: internal tracking, never posted.
- The one-shot push notifications to specific friend groups this cycle: those are real
  people's real rolls and check-ins, not feature material, and never belong in public copy.
