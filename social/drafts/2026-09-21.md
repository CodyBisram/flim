# FLIM social drafts, week of Sep 21

Covers 1.5.3 going live: the follow-only page, safe-on-capture uploads, bigger
accessibility targets, and a posted notice that lands you in the right place. Also
what's building next, starting with the camera asking for its own permission.

## X

### 1. Your page is for the people who follow you
> Your page is for the people who follow you. Anyone on FLIM can find you and follow, and your photographs show up for them from that point on, not before. Being tagged in someone else's photo still lets you see it either way.

Why now: shipped in 1.5.3, live now. A privacy shift worth explaining properly, held for this exact post.

### 2. Safe the instant you take it
> A photo writes to your phone the moment you take it, before it waits its turn to upload. It keeps uploading even if you lock the screen right after. If FLIM can't keep a shot, it says so until it's safe.

Why now: shipped in 1.5.3, closing the failure mode described as building last cycle.

### 3. Bigger buttons, brighter text
> Every button in FLIM is now at least 44 points to tap. The largest accessibility text sizes are supported, color names read aloud to VoiceOver, and the faintest text got brighter.

Why now: shipped in 1.5.3. Unglamorous, but it matters most to the people it helps.

### 4. Building: the camera asks for itself
> Building: camera access used to get asked for during sign-up, before you'd even seen the app. Now the camera asks the first time you open it, when the ask actually makes sense. Say no and there's a way back to the feed, not a dead end.

Why now: part of the redesign work in progress, worth explaining before it ships.
