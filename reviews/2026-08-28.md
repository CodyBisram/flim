# Review notes for Aug 28

Covers commits 62a5390..d25f800 on main (last 24 hours).

## 1. RollDetailView.swift:366 (high)

The reveal re-presents itself on every reappearance of RollDetailView once its `.task` restarts, because nothing in this diff replaces the on-present write of `revealSeenKey` that used to make the check idempotent.

Failure scenario: open a developed, unwatched roll. The `.task` at line 366 sets `showReveal = true` since `revealSeenKey` is unset. The reader taps X in `RollRevealView` (which deliberately does not call `onCompleted`, so the flag stays unwritten, per the comment at RollRevealView.swift:118-120). `RollDetailView`'s own comment at line 313-314 states its `.task` is auto-cancelled the moment the view disappears; a `fullScreenCover` presentation triggers that disappear/reappear cycle on the presenter. When the cover dismisses, `.task` reruns from the top, reaches the same check with the flag still `false`, and slams the reveal back up automatically. This repeats on every reappearance (including after dismissing the `PhotoPagerView` cover at line 404), until the reader happens to page all the way through the reveal or tap Done.

## 2. RollRevealView.swift:134-137 (medium)

The pager tags pages by array index while `RollRevealViewModel.skipDeadFrame` (RollRevealViewModel.swift:293-305) mutates `deck` and clamps `index`, but the view's own `selection` state is never resynced to the clamped `viewModel.index`.

Failure scenario: deck `[A,B,C]`, reader on C (`selection == 2`, `viewModel.index == 2`). C's rendition fails to load, calling `skipDeadFrame` (RollRevealView.swift:231). `deck` becomes `[A,B]`, `viewModel.index` clamps to 1, but `selection` stays 2, a tag no page carries any more. `onChange(of: selection)` never fires so `moved(to:)` is never called, the credit line ("N of M", RollRevealView.swift:357) reads off `viewModel.index` while the pager shows a different frame, and `isCurrent` (`index == selection`, line 301) matches nothing in the rack scrubber. Removing a dead frame before the current one is quieter but still wrong: `index`/`selection` are left unchanged, silently advancing the reader one frame past the photo they were looking at and burning that frame's one-time develop beat off screen.

## 3. RollRevealView.swift:387 (low)

`RollRevealView` stages an undoable report through `UndoCenter` but has no `.undoCapsuleHost` of its own, unlike `PhotoPagerView` (PhotoPagerView.swift:337), which carries one specifically because it too is presented as a fullScreenCover.

Failure scenario: tap the flag on someone else's frame during a reveal. The only registered host is `MainTabView.swift:184`, which is fully covered by the reveal's own fullScreenCover, so neither the "Reported" capsule nor its Undo button is reachable during the 5s window. The report commits unconditionally at expiry with no visible confirmation, and if `reportPhoto` fails and `revert` fires, the flag silently un-fills with no explanation shown.

## 4. SharePreviewSheet.swift:310-313 (low)

`ShareLink` is built from `outgoing`, which falls back to the raw 3:4 photo while `storyImage`/`fullImage` are still rendering in the `.task` at line 116, and nothing disables the button until they land.

Failure scenario: a reader whose remembered `shareExportFormat` is `.story` or `.full` opens the sheet and taps Share before the sequential print -> story -> fill render chain reaches their format. The button reads "Share story" or "Share full frame" and the preview shows a held 9:16 box, but `SharedPhoto(image: outgoing)` still carries the unimprinted, uncropped 3:4 master, so the file that actually leaves does not match what the button and preview promised.
